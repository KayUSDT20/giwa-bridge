How a deposit works

GIWA is an OP Stack chain, so deposits use the standard OptimismPortal flow — the same one in the official guide.

01

Lock on L1

Your ETH is sent to the OptimismPortal contract on Ethereum Sepolia (0x9569…C78A), which emits a TransactionDeposited event.

02

Sequencer relays it

GIWA's sequencer reads the L1 event, waits ~4–5 blocks so a reorg can't undo it, and derives the matching deposit transaction on L2.

03

Mint on L2

The same amount of ETH is credited to your GIWA Sepolia address. Total time is typically 1–3 minutes. No challenge period — that's withdrawals, which this tool doesn't do.

Good to know

How long does a deposit take?

About 1–3 minutes. The L1 transaction confirms in ~12 seconds, then GIWA's sequencer waits roughly 4–5 L1 blocks before processing the deposit, as a safeguard against L1 reorgs.

Why deposits only?

Deposits are the fast, simple direction — one transaction, a few minutes. Withdrawals back to Sepolia are a three-step process (initiate, prove, finalize) with a ~7-day challenge period. This tool stays focused on getting ETH onto GIWA.

What does it cost?

Only the Sepolia gas for one L1 transaction, plus the ETH you deposit. The deposit itself sets a 200,000 L2 gas limit, which is covered by the deposit — you don't pay L2 gas separately. Testnet ETH has no monetary value.

Is this mainnet?

No. GIWA mainnet is under development, so this bridge works on testnet only: Ethereum Sepolia → GIWA Sepolia. Never send mainnet ETH to these contract addresses.
